## Mitigation & Review Policy

1. All **Critical** and **High** risks must have automated test coverage (both positive and negative test cases) prior to closing.
2. Changes mitigating security risks (RSK-01, RSK-02, RSK-03) must not degrade performance for valid worker nodes.
3. Every stage report must re-evaluate the risk register and confirm that residual risk levels remain acceptable.
